🔥 Heat-seal Film

▼

Heat-seal film is one of our core products, made from high-quality resin materials through precision processing. This product offers excellent heat-sealing performance, with strong seals and good airtightness, effectively preventing moisture, leakage, and contamination. It also features high transparency and good toughness, making it suitable for automatic packaging equipment to improve production efficiency. Widely used in packaging applications across food, pharmaceutical, daily chemical, and electronic component industries. Thickness, width, and roll length can be customized according to customer requirements to flexibly adapt to different packaging scenarios.

🏷️ Custom Label Printing

▼

Specialized Custom Label Printing on Film Materials — Performance for Your Products

We specialize in custom label printing on various film materials, ideal for applications requiring excellent water resistance, temperature tolerance, chemical durability, and long-lasting performance. Film labels are widely used in electronics, household chemicals, pharmaceuticals, outdoor gear, industrial manufacturing, and many other fields where harsh environments demand reliable labeling.

Available Film Materials

PET (Polyester) Resistant to high temperatures, acids, alkalis, and tearing. Suitable for electronics, batteries, outdoor equipment.
PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride) Excellent flexibility and vibrant color reproduction. Ideal for cosmetics, toys, decorative stickers.
BOPP (Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene) High transparency, waterproof and oil-proof. Commonly used for bottle labeling on food, beverages, and daily chemical products.
PE (Polyethylene) Soft and squeezable, perfect for containers like detergent and shampoo bottles that require squeezing.
PP (Polypropylene) Chemically resistant, suitable for medical devices, laboratory instruments, and certain industrial products.
