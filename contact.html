Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ varies by product type. For standard films, MOQ typically starts at 1-3 tons. For custom label printing, we accept both trial runs and mass production orders. Contact us with your needs for exact details.

Do you provide free samples?

Yes, we provide samples for quality evaluation. Customer covers shipping costs. We can send A4-size samples or small rolls depending on the product.

What are your payment terms?

We typically accept T/T (Telegraphic Transfer) with 30% deposit and 70% balance before shipment. Other terms can be discussed for long-term partnerships.

How long is the production lead time?

Standard products: 7-15 days. Custom specifications or large orders: 15-30 days. We always strive for on-time or early delivery.

Can you handle export documentation and shipping?

Yes, we have extensive export experience to 30+ countries. We handle all documentation (CO, Form A, packing list, invoice) and can arrange shipping via sea freight, air freight, or express courier.
